Stage 02

Understand papers through clear AI explanations

Each paper gets a structured read — aim, method, findings, limitations — and you can ask follow-up questions answered from the paper’s own text.

{% include "landing/_icon.html" with name="book" %} summary · Roediger & Karpicke, 2006
Method

Two experiments; students studied prose then either re-read it or took free-recall tests. Final test at 5 minutes and 1 week.

Findings

Repeated testing produced much better week-later recall than repeated studying, despite lower confidence at the time.

Limitations

Prose passages in a lab; transfer to open-ended exam formats is left to later work.

  • QWhat was the retention interval?
  • AFive minutes and one week after study — see Experiment 1.§2.1
