{% include "landing/_icon.html" with name="book" %} explain · Sweller (1988), §4
Original

“Element interactivity imposes intrinsic load that is irreducible without a change in the learner’s schema-based knowledge.”

Plain language

Some material is hard because its parts only make sense together. That difficulty only drops once the learner has built the right mental model.

QDoes this apply to novices and experts equally?—
ANo — the paper argues load is higher for novices who lack schemas.§4.2
